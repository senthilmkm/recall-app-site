Privacy Policy for Recall

Effective Date: February 04, 2026

1. Overview

Recall is an offline-first personal intelligence application designed with privacy as its core principle. We believe your memories belong to you alone.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share your personal data (photos, notes, keys). All data remains locally on your device or your personal iCloud. Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal names, photos, voice notes, or item details to any external server. All data is stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud (if enabled by you). Usage Data: We do not track your behavior or usage of the App.

3. In-App Purchases

Transactions are processed securely by Apple. We do not store payment info.

4. Permissions

FaceID/TouchID: Used solely for unlocking the secure "Which" vault. Biometric data never leaves your device's Secure Enclave. Camera/Microphone: Used only to create memory attachments at your request.
