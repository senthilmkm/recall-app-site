Privacy Policy

Recall is an offline-first personal intelligence application designed with privacy as its core principle. We believe your memories belong to you alone.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share your personal data (photos, notes, keys). All data remains locally on your device that includes your data locally secured in Vault.

  • Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal names, photos, voice notes, or item details to any external server. All data is stored locally on your device that includes your data locally secured in Vault.
  • Usage Data: We do not track your behavior or usage of the App.

2. In-App Purchases

Transactions are processed securely by the Apple App Store. We do not process or store your credit card information. We only receive a confirmation receipt from Apple to unlock Pro features.

3. Permissions

  • FaceID / TouchID: Used solely for unlocking the secure "Which" vault. Biometric data never leaves your device's Secure Enclave.
  • Camera / Microphone: Used only to create memory attachments at your express request.
