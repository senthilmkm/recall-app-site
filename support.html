Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 Is my data private?

Yes. Recall is strictly offline-first. Your photos, voice notes, and memories are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your data to any external server. If you enable iCloud backups, your data is encrypted by Apple.

🔄 How do I restore my purchase?

If you upgraded to Recall Pro and reinstalled the app, you don't need to pay again. Simply go to the upgrade screen and tap the "Restore Previous Purchase" link at the bottom. The app will verify your receipt and unlock Pro instantly.
